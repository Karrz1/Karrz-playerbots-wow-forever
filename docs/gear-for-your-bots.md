# Gear For Your Bots


### Regear
Click **Regear all** in the **/fb** window, or type `/fb regear all`. Your online bots get their gear re-rolled so they don't all wear the same pieces. Better items they already wear are kept. Replaced ones go into their bags.

### Gear modes
You will pick how well geared your bots are. Say it in party chat (`gear t3`) and it applies to all your bots.

- **gear match**: the default. Below level 60 the bots wear good quest and dungeon gear for their level. At 60 they wear gear about as strong as yours.
- **gear best**: the best gear the server allows for their level, whatever you wear.
- **gear bis**: best-in-slot raid gear at level 60.
- **gear t1**: Tier 1 sets (Molten Core).
- **gear t2**: Tier 2 sets (Blackwing Lair).
- **gear t3**: Tier 3 sets (Naxxramas).

Below level 60 the tier and best-in-slot modes act like **match**.

Gear for world and battleground bots is not changed by this. Your own bots get the better gear.

*The server owner can limit which modes are allowed.*
