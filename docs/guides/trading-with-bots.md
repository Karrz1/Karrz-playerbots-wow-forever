# Trading With Bots


### See what a bot has
Whisper any bot **what do you have** (or "what are you selling"). It whispers back a numbered list of the things it is willing to sell, each with its price.

### Buy from a bot
1. Whisper **what do you have**.
2. Whisper **buy 2** (the number from the list) or **buy linen cloth** (the name).
3. The bot tells you the price. Open a trade with it and put exactly that much gold in.
4. The bot puts the item in its side. Press Accept and the deal is done.
If you put in less than the price, the bot just tells you the price again.

### Sell to a bot
Open a trade, put an item in, and leave your side's gold empty. The bot puts gold in its side and whispers what it offers. Accept if you like the price. Fair means the Auction House value of the item; if the item has no Auction House price, it is based on what a vendor would charge for it (a grey item is worth what a vendor pays). A bot never pays more than it has, and refuses things that are worth nothing.

### Gifts to your own bots
Gear that is an upgrade for your bot, handed to it in a trade, is still a free gift, and so is gold. They wear the better item and keep the old one in their bags. Anything else you put in a trade with a bot is bought at the fair price.

### Bag space
Bots clear out junk (grey items, materials they can't use, gear they can't wear) to keep a few bag slots free, so you should not find a bot that can't take your trade. If a bot's bags really are full you will get "my bags are full".

*Prices are set by the server, so the same item is the same price from every bot.*
