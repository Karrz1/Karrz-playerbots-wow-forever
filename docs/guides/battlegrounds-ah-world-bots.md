# Battlegrounds, Auction House and World Bots


### Battlegrounds
- Queue as normal. The server fills **both teams** with bots, so matches start quickly.
- Matches fill while they run: bots join a match that has already started, and every level bracket is covered, twinks included. Several battlegrounds can run at once.
- Bots play the objective: they capture, defend, escort flag carriers, stop enemy captures, call out nodes and camp the enemy graveyard in Arathi Basin. They are aggressive and mix classes, with few healers.
- Bots mount up in the start area, and dead bots use their own team's spirit guide.
- Real players come first. If a team is full, a bot gives up its place to make room for you.
- Your own party bots are not used to fill battleground teams.

### Auction House
- The auction house is stocked with items so there is always something to buy and sell. Items are classic ones up to level 60.
- Prices are fair, based on vendor values.
- Bots buy player listings too. A reasonably priced item sells within a few minutes. Very overpriced items won't sell.
- Listings show a seller name, like a real player.

### World bots
- You will see other characters questing, fighting and travelling in every zone. Many are bots.
- They answer a hello or a short question when you are close (/say), and reply to whispers.
- They are polite: they decline most group invites unless free, and accept a duel now and then. You can buy from them and sell to them (see **Trading With Bots**).
- Only the owner can order a bot. A world bot won't take your commands.
