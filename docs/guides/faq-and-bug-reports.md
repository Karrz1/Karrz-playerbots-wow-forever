# FAQ and Reporting Bugs

## Part 1 of 2: Questions

**How do I open the WoW Forever Bots window?** Type **/fb**.

**My bot ignores me.** Only its owner can order it, and it must be in your group. Try **Come**, then **Follow**.

**How many bots can I have?** Up to 5 of your own bots in the open world. Inside a dungeon or raid, the instance size sets the limit (5, 10, 20 or 40).

**The Bots window doesn't show up.** The add-on may be switched off. At the character screen, click AddOns and tick **WoW Forever Bots**, plus "Load out of date AddOns".

**How do I get my bots back after a log-out?** Click **Call saved**. Use **Save group** first so they're on your list.

**Bots run out of mana or die a lot.** Make sure you have a healer, watch the healers' mana before each pull (the bots do not hold a pull back for it), and put the bots on **Passive** in dungeons.

## Part 2 of 2: How to report a bug

Found something odd? Tell us. A good report lets us fix it quickly.

### Include:
1. **What you did**, step by step, just before it went wrong.
2. **What you expected** and **what happened instead**.
3. **A screenshot** if you can. Include the chat window and your bot's status line.
4. **Bot names**, classes and roles involved, for example "Raveni, Priest, Heal".
5. **Where you were**: the zone or dungeon, and roughly when (time and time zone).
6. **Group size**: 5-man or raid, and how many bots.
7. **Which order or button** you used last: Pull, Pull back, Fall back, Release and so on.

**Tip:** whisper the bot `who` or `stats` and paste the answer. It helps us see what the bot thought it was doing.

**Post it in:** the #bug-report channel on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/Jk596N3xun) (or #install-help for setup questions). Please use one post per problem.

### Before you post
- Check the FAQ above.
- Try **Come**, then **Follow**.
- Say if it happens every time or only once.

Thank you. Reports from players are how the bots get better!
