# Dungeon Tips


- **Let the tank pull.** Use **Pull** so the tank gets the monster's attention first. The damage dealers wait for your **Attack**, or join by themselves after 6 seconds. Use **Pull back** if you want the tank to bring the monster to you.
- **Watch the healers' mana.** Casters and healers need mana to keep you alive. After a fight the bots drink and eat on their own, but they do not hold your pull back for mana: check the healers before you pull.
- **Use Passive.** Passive bots stay close, defend you and do as told, and do not pull extra monsters while you walk. Switch to Aggressive only if you want them to attack on their own.
- **Set the rally away from the next pack.** Click **Set rally** in a safe spot, well back from the next group of monsters. Then **Pull back** and **Fall back** bring fights to a place that won't wake anything else.
- **Follow stays in charge.** While Follow is on, bots only defend. They won't push forward or pull until you say **Attack**, **Pull** or **Resume**.
- **Your marks rule.** Put a **skull** on what must die first and a **cross** on the next. The bots do exactly what your marks say and only choose on monsters you did not mark.
- **Crowd control.** Put a crowd-control mark (such as moon) on a monster, or assign a Mage, Warlock, Priest or Rogue to an icon, and they keep that monster controlled while the rest fight. Unmarked monsters that a bot can control are controlled automatically.

- **Check your raid lockouts.** Open Social (**O**), then **Raid**, then **Raid Info** to see which raids you are locked to.

### If you wipe
1. Click **Release**. Every dead bot releases its spirit and runs back to its body. Inside a dungeon they walk back in through the portal.
2. If a healer survives, bots wait for a resurrection instead of releasing.
3. Once they're back, click **Follow** or **Call saved** to regroup, then **Set rally** again before the next pull.
4. If a bot is lost or stuck, click **Come** to bring it to you.
