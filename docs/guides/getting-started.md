# Getting Started


This server has **WoW Forever Bots**: computer-controlled characters that play like real people. Some wander the world, fight, quest and trade on their own. Others are **your own party members**, and you tell them what to do.

**Bots are on by default.** You don't need to install a bot program or log in a second account.

### Open the Bots window
Type **/fb** in chat. A window opens with buttons for everything below. It stays where you drag it and stays open when you relog. Type /fb again, or click the X, to close it.  
*(If the window does not appear, the add-on is missing: see [Players: get the add-on](../../README.md#players-get-the-add-on) in the main README, or ask on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/Jk596N3xun). It is the small **WoW Forever Bots** add-on.)*

### Your first steps
1. Open **/fb**.
2. Click a **class button** and pick a role (**Tank**, **Heal** or **DPS**). A bot of that class joins your group at your level, already geared.
3. Add a few more until you have a party.
4. Click **Follow** and go play. The bots follow you and defend themselves.
5. When you want a fight, target a monster and click **Attack** or **Pull**.

### Good to know
- Only *you* can give orders to your bots.
- In the open world you can have up to 5 of your own bots out at once.
- **Save group** remembers your bots. Next time, **Call saved** brings them all back to you.
- Use the buttons to add and order your bots. You can also right-click a bot's portrait and choose **Invite**.
- Your own bots greet you like a friend when you log in.
